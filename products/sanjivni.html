How to Use

1. Add 1 scoop (approx. 30g) of Sanjivni detergent to 5 litres of water.

2. Soak clothes for 20–30 minutes for herbal action to work.

3. Gently rub the fabric to remove stains.

4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.

5. Dry in shade to preserve fabric quality.
