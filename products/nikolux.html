How to Use

1. Add 1 scoop (approx. 30g) of Nikolux detergent to 5 litres of water.

2. Soak clothes for 15–20 minutes for best results.

3. Gently rub the fabric to remove tough stains.

4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.

5. Dry in shade for best color preservation.
