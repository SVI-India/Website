About Siddhi Vinayak Enterprises

Our journey of excellence in detergent manufacturing

Since 2009, Siddhi Vinayak Enterprises has been crafting premium quality detergents that families across India trust. From our humble beginnings in Lachharsar, Rajasthan, we have grown into a leading manufacturer committed to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction.

Our Story

What began as a small-scale detergent production unit in Lachharsar has today grown into one of Rajasthan's most trusted detergent manufacturers. Our founders envisioned a brand that delivers international quality at affordable prices — and that vision continues to drive us every single day.

From selecting the finest raw materials to packaging that preserves freshness, every step of our process reflects our obsession with quality and customer trust.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality cleaning solutions to every Indian household while maintaining the highest standards of manufacturing excellence and environmental responsibility.

We continuously invest in modern technology, quality testing, and sustainable practices to ensure that every product we ship reflects our core values.

Our Vision

To become India's leading detergent brand recognized globally for quality, innovation, and customer trust.

We aim to expand our reach to every corner of India and beyond, while staying true to our roots — affordable, honest, and quality-first.
