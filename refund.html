Refund Policy

Simple and transparent refund process

📅 Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to all orders.

2. Eligibility for Refund

Refunds may be issued if:

  • Damaged on arrival
  • Wrong product delivered
  • Manufacturing defect

3. Non-Refundable Cases

No refunds for:

  • Change of mind
  • Buyer-caused damage
  • Opened products

4. Refund Process

Follow these steps:

  1. Report issue to orders@sve-in.xubi.org
  2. Verification within 3–5 business days
  3. Refund processed in 7–10 business days
  4. Refund via original method or bank transfer

5. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds depend on damage extent.

6. Cancellation Policy

  • Cancel before dispatch — free
  • After dispatch — subject to conditions
  • Refund in 5–7 business days

7. Return Shipping

Return shipping depends on the case.
